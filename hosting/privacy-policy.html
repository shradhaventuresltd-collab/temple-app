2. What the app does

Temple Directory India is a free, public directory of temples and related heritage sites in India. You can browse, search and open temple pages (history, timings, address, photos and directions). You do not need an account, and the app has no sign-in.

In short, what is processed and why:

  • Advertising ID / app set ID, IP-derived approximate location, ad interactions and ad-SDK diagnostics: collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud (section 4).
  • IP address and standard technical request data: received by Google Firebase and Google Fonts when the app downloads temple content and fonts (section 5).
  • Your email address and message, only if you email us: to answer you and keep a record of the request (section 8).
  • Ad consent choices, and your acceptance of the Terms when the first-launch acceptance screen is available: stored on your device only.

We do not collect your name, phone number, contacts, precise location, photos, financial information, passwords, health or biometric data, or any other "sensitive personal data or information" under India's Information Technology Rules, 2011.

3. Information we do not collect

  • No accounts. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, password or a user ID, and the app has no sign-up or sign-in.
  • No device location. The app does not request location permission and does not read your GPS.
  • No uploads or posts. You cannot upload photos or post reviews, comments or messages in the app. The app does not request camera, photo or storage permissions.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs of our own. The app does not include Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics or any other analytics or crash-reporting SDK. If we add one, we will update this policy and our Google Play Data safety answers before the version that contains it is released.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner ad on the temple list, and from time to time a full-screen (interstitial) ad when you open temple pages.

What Google receives. When ads are requested, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares with Google, and with Google's advertising partners, information such as:

  • your device's advertising ID and app set ID,
  • approximate location derived from your IP address,
  • ad interactions (for example ad views and taps), and
  • diagnostic and performance information about the ad SDK.

Google uses this information for advertising, ad measurement and analytics, and fraud prevention and security. We do not receive your advertising ID linked to your identity, and we do not sell personal information. To learn how Google uses this data, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Consent and your choices. Before ads load, the app checks with Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP):

  • Where Google requires consent (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland), the app shows Google's consent form. Your choices are stored on your device and applied to ad requests. If you do not give the consent the form needs, the app does not request ads. You can change your choices later from Ad privacy choices in the app menu.
  • Where consent is not required, ads may be personalised; there is no separate opt-in for personalised ads in that case.
  • If the consent check fails or times out, the app keeps working and only requests non-personalised ads.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads, depending on your device).

5. Temple content and Google Firebase

Temple information (name, location, deity, history, timings, address, map coordinates) and temple photos are published by us and stored on Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Cloud Storage). The app downloads this content to show it to you. It does not send any information you enter to Firebase, and visitors cannot change it. Temple photos may be cached on your device so they load faster; you can clear the cache by clearing the app's storage in Android settings. Google Firebase receives standard technical information, such as your IP address, to deliver this content.

The app downloads its fonts from Google Fonts (a Google service) over HTTPS while it runs. Like any web request, this sends your IP address and standard request data (such as the requested font and your device's browser/user-agent details) to Google. It does not send anything you enter in the app.

6. Directions (Google Maps)

When you tap Get Directions, the app opens Google Maps (or another app that can handle the link) using the temple's published coordinates. The app does not send your location. What you do in Google Maps is governed by Google's terms and privacy policy.

7. Security and international transfers

All data the app sends or receives (Firebase, AdMob and Google Fonts) is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. Google may process data on servers outside India under its own policies. If we become aware of a personal data breach that affects you, we will inform you and the authorities as the law requires.

8. Retention

We do not keep a profile about you. Advertising data is retained by Google under Google's policies. Emails you send us are kept only as long as needed to answer you and keep a record of the request.

9. Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information, email shradhaventuresltd@gmail.com and we will remove it.

10. Your rights, consent and grievances

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (and, where they apply to you, other laws such as the GDPR), you can:

  • Access: ask for a summary of the personal data we process about you and how we use it.
  • Correction and erasure: ask us to correct, complete, update or delete your personal data.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, as easily as you gave it. For ads, use Ad privacy choices in the app menu (where shown) or reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings. You can also stop all processing by uninstalling the app. Withdrawal does not affect processing that already happened.
  • Grievance redressal: complain to us about how we handle your personal data.
  • Nominate another person to exercise your rights if you die or become incapacitated.
  • Complain to the Data Protection Board of India if you are not satisfied with our response. Under the Act, you must first use our grievance process.

How to make a request. Email shradhaventuresltd@gmail.com (or write to the Grievance Officer at the address in section 1) with the subject "Privacy request" and say what you want. The app has no accounts and we do not hold your name or contact details, so we cannot link app data to you. Data held by Google (such as advertising data) is tied to your device's advertising ID, which you control in Android settings. Google's own tools are at https://myadcenter.google.com. We will answer emails you send us about your data.

Response time. We will acknowledge your request or grievance within 24 hours and resolve it within 15 days of receiving it.
