Chabimura (Chakrakma) is a set of large rock-carved panels on a steep cliff above the Gomati river, about 7.5 km from Amarpur in Gomati district. The carvings show Shiva, Vishnu, Kartikeya, Mahishasuramardini Durga and other deities. Our sources do not confirm active worship here, so treat it mainly as a protected heritage monument. The site is also known as Devtamura, after the surrounding Devtamura hills.
